  - Replaced the per-call `new KotlinLazy<bool>(...)` for unitSpecificAllowOcean in CannotPassThroughReason with a private `UnitSpecificAllowOcean()` method computed directly at the two ocean branches. Added two tests: `CannotPassThroughReason_AllocatesNothingOnTheCommonPath` (GC.GetAllocatedBytesForCurrentThread == 0 over 10k land-tile calls after warmup) and `UnitSpecificOceanEntry_LetsOnlyMatchingUnitsThroughOcean` (the UnitsMayEnterOcean filter lets matching units through ocean and blocks non-matching).
