- Notes:
  - Audit findings: C036's turn bar drew connection markers from the last LobbyState, but a game started through ServerLobby created its GameHost with announceRoster false, and resume/promotion paths did the same; the roster (connected flags) therefore never updated after Start, and TurnTimer knew the drop grace but no client could show it. NetGameSession also surfaced raw session codes ("session-closed") in the game status line.
    Implemented:
    - TurnTimer.DropDeadline(civId) (disconnectedAt + DropGraceSeconds) and LobbySlot.GraceEndsAt, filled by GameHost.BuildRoster. Roster announcements are now on for every hosted-game path (ServerLobby start and resume, HostingSession resume, StandbyPromotion), so a drop/return reaches the turn bar without a command.
    - TurnBarModel: a dropped, unended seat reads "Name (offline, m:ss to reconnect)" with the host's grace deadline; TurnBarData.GraceCountdown lets GameScreen redraw once a second. Ended seats drop out of the countdown.
    - ClientSession: ReconnectAttempt/MaxReconnectAttempts/SecondTableToNextAttempt/SecondsToGiveUp (read on the session's own clock), and RejoinNow(): cancels the backoff and resets the attempt budget; the wait CTS is guarded by a lock so Cancel cannot race Dispose, and a Rejoin pressed while dialling is cleared on connect.
    - JoinModel/ReconnectOverlay: ConnectionView.Rejoin, ReconnectView (attempt, next try, election give-up) and ReconnectDetail; the overlay adds a Rejoin button, repeats the turn bar's waiting line while disconnected, and redraws once a second. App: while retrying Rejoin dials now; after give-up it disposes the session without Leave (the seat stays) and reopens the join screen with the last address.
    - Clean messages: gave-up, snapshot-rejected, internal-error, left and disposed in JoinModel.RefusalText; NetGameSession.SubmitAsync turns session-closed/connection-lost into sentences instead of codes.
    - Harness scenario `reconnect` (real loopback TCP, in-process host, two real clients, frozen host clock): kills the host player's connection and asserts the friend's bar shows "Waiting for Host (offline, 2:00 to reconnect), Friend" and clears it when restored; then kills the friend's connection, asserts the overlay's attempt/backoff/waiting line and the Rejoin button, clicks Rejoin and asserts the overlay goes away with the same seat. Goldens in golden/reconnect, screenshots.json T346 and SCREENSHOTS.md regenerated; C006's overlay golden/caption updated for the new button. Scenarios reconnect, join, mp-turn, next-turn, next-turn-long, relay-session and layout-matrix pass.
    - Tests: TurnTimer grace, TurnBarModel grace/ended, JoinModel reconnect/gave-up/rejoin/clean codes, ClientSession RejoinNow and countdowns, NetGameSession roster grace + WaitingFor, InProcessHost drop roster announcement, HostElection give-up countdown, NetLobby roster-after-start expectations.
    Assumptions/deviations: SecondsToNextAttempt reports the default backoff schedule (the delay delegate is pluggable; tests that need exact numbers assert only the default); the roster stays the carrier of presence and grace (no new TurnStatus message, so raw-protocol sequences of existing clients are unchanged); T340's split-brain warning is untouched.
  - Correction to the previous note's typo: the exposed member is `ClientSession.SecondsToNextAttempt` (not "SecondTableToNextAttempt").
