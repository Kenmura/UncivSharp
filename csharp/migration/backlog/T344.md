- Notes:
  - Audit of every on-screen number and the tooltips added. Added: (1) HUD top bar — Gold/Science/Culture/Faith per turn now carry the engine's itemised per-turn sources (new EmpireTopBarQuery.StatSources, the same StatSource shape the city screen uses) and show the shared TooltipModel.StatBreakdown as their tooltip; the non-yield entries (Happiness, Golden Age, resources, Turn) keep their plain label because they are stocks/counts, not per-turn yields. (2) Combat preview card — each fighter's strength number's tooltip is the combat-strength formula via new TooltipModel.CombatStrength (base strength, each listed modifier, then the engine's own final figure; the final figure is the engine's, so an unlisted factor cannot make the tooltip disagree with the card). (3) Overview stats page — the per-turn yield chips (Gold/Science/Culture/Faith per turn) now carry the same breakdown (new EmpireOverviewResult.StatSources -> StatsEntry.Sources/StatValue); stored Gold and Happiness have none. City screen already had the per-stat breakdown (C056); tech/policy node cards already had name+detail tooltips; diplomacy rows/buttons already had tooltips — none needed a change. StatBreakdown lists only sources that contribute to the stat and shows the engine's total (not the sum), so a bonus the sources do not itemise cannot make a tooltip disagree with the screen.
  - Touch alternative: reused the existing TooltipHost touch path (C100) — on a touch build a tap on a tip-bearing control (including the new TooltipText set here) shows it above the finger, a long press (0.5 s) on a button shows it without pressing. The new tooltips are Control.TooltipText, which that path resolves through the parents. Covered by the existing TouchOrdersScenario; no new touch scenario was needed.
  - Harness scenarios: TooltipsScenario asserts the top bar's per-turn yields carry their breakdown and the non-yield entries keep their label; UnitOrdersScenario asserts each strength number's tooltip is the combat formula; OverviewScenario asserts the stats page's per-turn yield chips carry the breakdown and a stored amount does not. No default capture changed, so no golden was re-recorded (screenshots.json has a {"none": reason} entry for T344).
  - Remaining gaps (audited, deliberately not tooltipped — the number is already explained by its context or is a stock/count with no meaningful breakdown): (a) Overview tables (cities Pop/Health/Buildings, units Health/Moves, resources Amount, ranking Value, politics Influence/Peace-treaty, spies rank) — per-cell numbers are explained by their column heading, a per-cell tooltip would repeat it; (b) Diplomacy info/modifier/quest lines, religion lines, politics lines, and the tech screen's header (science/turns) — full sentences that already embed and explain their numbers; (c) Policy screen culture bar and golden-age line — a caption plus the status tooltip already cover them. These are intentional omissions, not missing work.
